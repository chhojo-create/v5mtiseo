Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the weight range MTI can cast and machine?

MTI manufactures castings and machined components from 1 KG to 8,000 KG per piece.

Can MTI supply fully machined, ready-to-install parts?

Yes — MTI combines foundry operations with CNC turning centers, VMC, wire-cut, and grinding machines, plus in-house powder coating, CED coating, and phosphating, so parts arrive ready-to-install.

What finishing options does MTI offer?

Powder coating, CED coating, and phosphating are all performed in-house, alongside hydro and pressure testing.

What industry standards can MTI manufacture to?

MTI manufactures to domestic and international specifications including IS, ASME, and EN standards for both OEM and export customers.
