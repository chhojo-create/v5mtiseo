Who We Are

Six Decades of
Manufacturing Mastery

Mina Textile Industries (MTI) is an ISO 9001:2015-certified company that has grown from supplying raw textile castings to delivering precision-engineered power transmission products and industrial castings for OEMs worldwide.

Based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, MTI combines decades of expertise with modern manufacturing facilities — from induction melting to CNC machining and finishing — to serve diverse sectors including construction, agriculture, and energy.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Mina Textile Industries (MTI) manufacture?

MTI manufactures precision-engineered industrial castings, power transmission components such as pulleys and couplings, manifolds, valve parts, and fully machined, ready-to-install components for OEMs, in weights from 1 KG to 8,000 KG per piece. See the full product range.

Where is MTI located?

MTI's manufacturing facility is in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India — within one of India's largest foundry clusters. Full address and directions are on the Contact page.

How long has MTI been in business?

MTI was founded in 1964 and has over 60 years of continuous manufacturing experience, evolving from a textile-sector casting supplier into a diversified industrial component manufacturer.

Is MTI ISO certified?

Yes, MTI is ISO 9001:2015 certified and performs in-house metallurgical, dimensional, and mechanical testing, with access to third-party inspection facilities.

Does MTI supply export orders?

Yes. MTI manufactures to international standards including ASME and EN, and its Gujarat location provides efficient logistics for export shipments.
